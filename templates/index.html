Tiered Risk Analysis System

Moving beyond binary answers with clear, actionable threat categorization.

Low Risk 0 – 30%

Posting appears genuine. Standard professional markers, clear corporate domain names, and standard hiring structures identified.

Moderate Risk 31 – 70%

Contains vague company information or unusual phrasing. Proceed with caution and verify company credentials independently.

High Risk 71 – 100%

Strong indicators of scam activity. Flags unusual payment requests, unrealistic salaries, or off-platform communication traps.
